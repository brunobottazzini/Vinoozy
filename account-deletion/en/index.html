Vinoozy — Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Vinoozy account and associated data without having the app installed.

Send a deletion request by email

Write from the Google email address you use for Vinoozy, or identify the email address of the account concerned. Explicitly request deletion of your Vinoozy account and its data. Do not send passwords or unsolicited identity documents. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before proceeding.

Request deletion by email: bottazzinibruno@gmail.com

We normally respond within one month of receiving the request and explain any lawful extension. This request does not delete your Google account.

Data that will be deleted

Deletion removes your Vinoozy profile, personal cellar with its wines and History, and invitations and access records associated with your account. People you shared your own cellar with will lose access to it.

Data that may remain

Records and notes you entered in other people’s cellars may remain with persistent technical identifiers; they are not necessarily anonymous. You can request their removal in the same email. Copies exported by other users remain under their control. Provider diagnostic data and technical copies follow the retention periods described in the privacy notice.

Deletion from the app

If you have the app installed, you can start deletion under Profile → Delete account. The email request is also available without reinstalling the app.

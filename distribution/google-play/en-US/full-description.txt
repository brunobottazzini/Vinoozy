Vinoozy is a wine cellar manager for the bottles you own: keep your wine inventory, know when to drink each wine and find food pairing ideas, even offline.

Every wine gets an indicative drinking window (start, peak and end), estimated from its type, appellation, vintage and level. The Dashboard shows which wines are ready to open now and which ones to keep.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Offline wine inventory: add, edit and record drinks without a connection; everything syncs when you are back online.
• When to drink each wine: a drinking window with start, peak and end, which you can correct at any time.
• Wine label reading on your phone: photograph the front and back, use a barcode or import text from Google Lens. You always review the details before saving.
• Dashboard: wines ready now, wines to wait for and wines to drink soon, at a glance.
• Drinking history with ratings and notes: say how a bottle was and the estimate of the same wine is corrected.
• Food pairing suggestions for every wine.
• Shared cellar: invite people, read only or with editing.
• Vinoozy Plus: no wine limit and no ads.

Your first Google sign-in requires Internet; some searches also require a connection.

READ LABELS ON YOUR PHONE
Text recognition runs on your device. Online searches may send words and barcodes to Open Food Facts. Opening a photo in Google Lens requires your confirmation.

Drinking windows are indicative estimates. Storage, style and the condition of each bottle may change the result. Estimates are not a quality guarantee or an invitation to consume alcohol.

Vinoozy is intended for adults. It does not sell alcoholic drinks.

Interface available in 25 languages, including English.

Catalogue data from Open Food Facts under ODbL 1.0 and DbCL 1.0. Derived database and attribution: https://github.com/brunobottazzini/Vinoozy/tree/main/assets/wine_db
Appellations from eAmbrosia and the national registers listed in the data documentation. Estimates are produced by Vinoozy.

Prepared Console entry title: Vinoozy — Google review account
Prepared "Any other information" field (425/500 characters):
Skip the introduction, then tap Sign in with Google and use the demo account above. Internet is required for first sign-in. In Cellar, tap Add and use manual entry to add a fictional wine. Open its detail and record a bottle; inspect Dashboard and History. Cellar menu > People manages sharing and permissions. Profile contains sign-out, privacy and account deletion. No subscription, payment or location restriction applies.

The developer enters the demo credentials directly in Play Console. Keep
credentials out of this file. The detailed flow below also supports QA;
a second demo account is useful for testing both sides of cellar sharing.

1. Open Vinoozy. On first launch, continue through or skip the introduction.
2. Tap Sign in with Google and use the dedicated test account above. An
   internet connection is required for the first sign-in.
3. Dashboard shows an overview of the cellar. Open the Cellar tab, tap Add,
   choose manual entry, enter a fictional wine and vintage, and save.
4. Open the wine to inspect its indicative ageing window. Type, style,
   storage and available dates affect this estimate.
5. Record one bottle as drunk after confirming. Undo is available briefly;
   History shows the record and permits eligible users to edit notes/rating.
6. Already available cellar data can be viewed and edited offline. Changes
   synchronise after reconnecting.
7. In the cellar menu, People allows an owner to invite another Google
   account and select view/edit permissions. Use the second test account
   above on another device to verify sharing. The owner can revoke access;
   the invitee can leave the cellar.
8. Profile provides Sign out, Privacy policy and Delete account. Deletion
   requires confirmation and renewed Google sign-in. Use disposable test
   accounts for deletion; provide working replacements for future reviews.

No paid features, subscription or advertising is integrated in this build.
The app manages a private wine inventory; it does not sell alcoholic drinks.
